### 2.2 Lag features (same-slot, forecast-safe)
- Total lag features: **2** — lag_336h (14-day same-slot lag), lag_672h (28-day same-slot lag).
- `lag_336h` = the trips value **exactly 14 days (336 h) earlier**, same zone & hour.
- `lag_672h` = the trips value **exactly 28 days (672 h) earlier**, same zone & hour.
- Shorter lags (24/48 h) are *deliberately excluded*: beyond day 1 of a 14-day horizon their value is not known at forecast time.

### 2.3 Rolling / history features
- Rain rolling sums (weather): **rain_3h_sum, rain_6h_sum, rain_24h_sum** — 3 h, 6 h and 24 h cumulative rain leading into the hour.
- Trailing zone-level windows (days): **hist_zone_recent_7d, hist_zone_recent_14d, hist_zone_recent_28d, hist_zone_recent_56d**.
- Plus the expanding seasonal means (`hist_zone_dow_hour_mean`, `hist_zone_hour_mean`, `hist_zone_dow_mean`, `hist_zone_mean`), the 8-week 'current-regime' profile (`hist_zdh_recent8w`, `hist_zh_recent8w`), the relative recent-level ratios (`rel_recent_7d/14d/28d`) and the capped city `trend_index`.
